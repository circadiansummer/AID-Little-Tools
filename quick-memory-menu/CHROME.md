# Chrome

## Install

1. On the [repository page](https://github.com/circadiansummer/AID-Little-Tools), click **Code → Download ZIP**.
2. Extract the ZIP and keep the extracted folder.
3. In Chrome, open `chrome://extensions`.
4. Turn on **Developer mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked**.
6. Select the **quick-memory-menu** folder inside the extracted `AID-Little-Tools-main` folder.
7. Open or refresh your AI Dungeon adventure.

## Use

1. Receive an AI response in AI Dungeon.
2. Within 15 minutes, click **Memories** at the bottom left.
3. Type a keyword and click **Search**.
4. Click **Next** or **Previous** to move between matching memories.

After changing **Show unused**, **Timeline**, or **Relevance**, click **Search** again.
