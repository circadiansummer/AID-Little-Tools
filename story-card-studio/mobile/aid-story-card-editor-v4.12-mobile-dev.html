Visual editing for Random Encounters Config and Advanced RE Settings Story Cards. Changes are written directly to your local Story Cards and included when you export for AI Dungeon. Set up the Random Encounters custom event script via AI Dungeon or GitHub.

Overrides disabledNo Advanced cardNo Config card
Where your settings go: Changes to standard controls edit the Config Story Card. Changes to advanced numbers edit Advanced RE Settings. Cards are created only when their controls are edited. One-shot action flags begin off to avoid accidental changes. Nothing is sent to AI Dungeon until you export and import the resulting JSON.
